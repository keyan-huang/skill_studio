# Sample data

Made-up skills for demos and checks. No real people, teams, or documents.

- `sample-project/.github/skills/`: 6 skills in a sample project.
- `sample-personal/.copilot/skills/`: 4 skills in a sample personal folder.
- `studio-notes.json`: each skill's job and source, plus the answers that
  were planted on purpose (duplicates, overlaps, a clash, a gap, and sample
  goals with their expected mix).

Planted on purpose:

- **Duplicate:** `document-writer` is in both places (project copy is newer).
- **Overlaps:** three spec writers; two slide makers.
- **Clash:** `brand-voice` (warm and friendly) versus `concise-writing`
  (short and neutral).
- **Gap:** nothing covers summarizing user research.
